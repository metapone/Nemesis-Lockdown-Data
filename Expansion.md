# Achievements

## 5 Achievement cards

| Title | Requirement | Effect |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| IEEE | Be the first Character to kill an Intruder in an Emergency Staircase or an Elevator Room. | Discard this card to fully load a chosen Item. |
| Knowledge Hungry | Be the first Character to reach Knowledge 7. | Discard this card to peek at one Contingency token (regardless of Section) and one chosen CSS token. |
| Reinvigoration | Be the first Character to gain Knowledge using a Computer Action card. | Discard this card to draw 3 cards. For each Contamination drawn, discard it and draw another card instead. |
| Scrambled Eggs | Be the first Character to destroy an Egg. | Discard this card to gain 3 Knowledge. |
| The Archivist | Be the first Character to Craft an Item. | Discard this card to use the Room Action of any working Room on the board without paying its cost. |

All Achievement cards have the following rules on the back:

>During Setup, draw 2 Achievement cards and place them face-up next to the main board.
>
>Players may look at these cards at any moment.
>
>The first player who meets the requirement described in the upper part of the Achievement card gains that card and place it in their Inventory.
>
>The lower part of the card describes its effect and when it can be played.
